# The tech workforce is voting with its feet — 12,000 people left the US

**Over 12,000 Indian tech workers have returned from the US** amid visa uncertainty and layoffs. That's not a statistic, it's a signal: **when immigration policy and hiring freezes hit at the same time, talent relocates — and it does not come back on schedule**. For companies betting on global hiring, this is a cost you can't put in a slide deck. 🌏

Meanwhile the AI giants keep shipping: today's roundup spans **Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, Nvidia, eMudhra and SpaceX**. The pattern is clear — **the frontier firms are competing on distribution and trust, not just model size**. Follow the partnerships, not the benchmarks. 🚀

Government tech is wobbling: in the Philippines, **the Ombudsman suspended DICT Undersecretary Almirol** — a reminder that **digital-infrastructure agencies carry real political risk**, and procurement pipelines stall when they do. 🏛️

On demand, **New Zealand businesses say they're ready to spend but their profit outlook stays subdued**. Spending appetite without margin is the most dangerous combination in tech procurement: budgets get cut first. All sources are listed below.
