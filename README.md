# Washington bans DeepSeek while Wall Street bets on AI compute

**The US Department of Homeland Security says civilian agencies can't use DeepSeek.** That's a procurement decision dressed as a security one — and it tells you where the AI market is heading: **model choice is now geopolitical**, not technical. If your stack depends on government contracts, pick your vendors accordingly. 🏛️

Meanwhile the money is flowing to infrastructure. **AI stocks rebounded, with CoreWeave jumping on a Goldman Sachs upgrade.** Investors are no longer paying for chatbots; they're paying for **compute capacity and the balance sheets behind it**. 💰

Memory is the quiet battleground: **Micron's HBM is driving higher margins while Chinese rival CXMT scares it**. High-bandwidth memory is where the AI boom actually gets manufactured — watch this space more than the model announcements. 🔬

And in the real world, **robotic hair transplants are reshaping patient experience in India** — automation arriving as a service industry story, not a Silicon Valley one. A $140M fintech loan expansion in the Philippines rounds out the picture: **AI and robotics are becoming boring, profitable plumbing**. All sources are listed below.
