# A quiet Sunday: Google pays its CEO's old school, and an AC start-up sells the bill, not the box

**Google is giving IIT Kharagpur $5 million** for advanced AI research and talent, focused on **Indic languages, agriculture and education**. Sundar Pichai says the institute **"shaped my own"** path. 🎓

**$5 million is a small cheque for Alphabet.** It buys goodwill and a talent pipeline more than breakthroughs, and that is fine.

The sharper story is **Optimist**, a Bengaluru AC start-up. It spent **two and a half years on R&D**, redesigned **heat exchangers and compressor control logic** for Indian heat and humidity, and says **80-85% of an AC's lifetime cost is electricity**. **Selling the power bill, not the box, is the right pitch.** ❄️

And after last week's outages, even BuzzFeed is telling readers how to **prepare for the next big internet outage**. 🔌 All sources are listed below.
