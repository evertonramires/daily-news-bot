# AI customer service is failing at the one job it was sold for: talking to people

Consumers are getting **error messages from AI support bots** instead of answers. That is the whole story in one line: companies deployed chatbots to cut headcount, and **the bots still can't handle a real complaint**. 🤖 If your automation produces a loop of unhelpful replies, you haven't saved costs — you've moved them onto the customer.

Patents are the new battleground for medtech: **Align Technology beat Angelalign in a patent infringement case at the Unified Patent Court**. Whoever owns the IP owns the market — and the UPC is now a venue worth taking seriously. ⚖️

On hardware, the difference between **TCL's QM7K and QM8K mini-LED TVs** is a reminder that "AI picture enhancement" marketing hides very ordinary panel specs. Compare the hardware, ignore the buzzwords.

Real tech change is still happening far from Silicon Valley: a **17-year-old in rural Ontario runs robotics camps for 1,200 kids** and is building a wider STEM access project. That is the pipeline nobody funds properly. 🎓

And **BLS International** keeps digitising government-to-citizen services — unglamorous, but where tech actually touches millions of lives. All sources are listed below.
