# The first fully AI-generated movie flopped — and that's the most useful data point of the day

**China's first fully AI-generated film failed at the box office**, which is not a verdict on AI filmmaking but on **what audiences will pay for when the "AI" label is the whole selling point**. Hype is a marketing budget, not a product. The same logic applies to every "AI-native" product being shipped this quarter: **the demo sells, the retention does not.**

Two quieter stories matter more than they look. A **CCS project that passed every technical test but failed the investment test** is the template for deep tech: engineering is rarely the bottleneck, **capital discipline is**. And Nvidia's boss being swept into an espionage story about his wife shows how fast **geopolitics attaches itself to hardware supply chains** — reputation risk is now supply-chain risk.

Also today: Landis+Gyr reported FY2026 half-year numbers, and India's Pixxel opened a US subsidiary to grow its satellite business. All sources are listed below.
