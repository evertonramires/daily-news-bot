# 🇮🇳 India's Quiet Tech Story Isn't in the Cities

Four of today's five headlines point the same way: **India is industrializing from the ground up**.

🚁 **Drone Didi** turning rural women into agri-drone operators is the most underrated tech policy anywhere — it skips the "digital literacy" debate and hands people **capital equipment**.

⛏️ The Patna roadshow for critical mineral blocks matters more than it sounds: **no minerals, no batteries, no defence electronics**.

✈️ A UP defence-corridor firm flying an **indigenous jet-powered loitering munition** shows the corridor model is producing hardware, not press releases.

📈 Kingfa's 111% profit jump is the boring proof — **specialty materials demand is real**.

⚰️ And the 444-mark student choosing funeral management? Honestly, respect. **Recession-proof.**

My take: the drone-plus-minerals-plus-defence stack is one policy, not three. 🔗
