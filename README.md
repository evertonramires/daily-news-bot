# AI companies are planning how to share the money — before the IPOs arrive

**Ahead of massive IPOs, AI companies are sketching plans to share their wealth.** Read that carefully: pre-listing, they are already working out how to split gains. **Who gets equity and who gets a salary is the real AI story of 2026** — the public markets will just price what insiders already decided. 💸

Government tech spending is under scrutiny: **FEMA scrapped plans for high-tech jets amid questions about DHS money spent on planes that were never used.** That's the pattern to watch — **procurement, not innovation, is where public tech budgets leak**. 🕊️

On the industrial side, **Renault brought hybrid E-TECH technology to India with the new Duster**, and **Sicona is putting $10.3 million into US battery technology while Himadri expands Birla Tyres by ₹500 crore**. The interesting signal: **the AI money eventually flows into batteries, chips and manufacturing** — not just software. 🔋

Diplomatic tech ties matter too: **Sheikh Abdullah joined US and Indian counterparts for talks in Mumbai**, the kind of meeting where compute, chips and standards get negotiated quietly. All sources are listed below.
