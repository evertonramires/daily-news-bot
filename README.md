# Washington now has an AI prosecutor — and it renamed the technology first

**The DOJ launched a "Super Intelligence Task Force" on 5 November**, by an Attorney General memo of 13 October, led by Associate Attorney General Stanley Woodward and Associate Deputy AG Stephen Weiss. It coordinates **criminal and civil** AI enforcement across US Attorneys' Offices and links the DOJ to the White House Super Intelligence Force, chaired by **ex-Goldman Sachs CEO Jay Clayton**.

The signal: **enforcement has caught up with the infrastructure.** A standing task force means regulators expect real cases — fraud, foreign interference, attacks on critical infrastructure — not policy papers. Two days earlier, New York prosecutors charged three people over AI-impersonated executives in a **$129M** scheme spanning July 2024 to February 2026.

The naming is not cosmetic: **Trump said in October he would ban "artificial intelligence" in official documents**, and the DOJ release says "SI". A name decides **who gets a mandate**. All sources are listed below.
