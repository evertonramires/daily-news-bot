# Nvidia's $185 billion lesson: survival is a product decision

**Nvidia nearly went bankrupt in 1996.** One risky bet — the path that built today's AI dominance — saved it, and made **Jensen Huang worth $185 billion**. The takeaway isn't luck. Companies that survive near-death moments are the ones that **bet on a technical direction early**, not the ones with the biggest balance sheet. 🧠

Meanwhile "Pax Silica" is being sold as a chip-order for the Philippines, with the **private sector cast as the key player**. Trade blocs built around semiconductors are where industrial policy is actually being written now — governments delegate, companies decide.

Not every tech story is a growth story: **Klook is withdrawing its US listing**, and **Snabbit posted a ₹168 crore loss on ₹12 crore of revenue** in FY26. Service-marketplaces burn cash chasing scale. Delisting is what happens when the math stops working. 💸

And real innovation still comes from unglamorous places: algae helped **Nobel winners develop new technology**. The next breakthrough rarely arrives with a keynote. 🔬
