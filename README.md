# AI found the PHP flaw; the human who leaked it is the real story

**The Guardian gives credit to AI for uncovering a PHP flaw and discredit to the leaker** — a small story with a sharp lesson: **the bottleneck in software security is no longer finding bugs, it is deciding who is allowed to say them out loud.** When discovery gets cheap and disclosure gets political, **processes built for a slower world break first**.

The macro signal is harder to ignore: the **ECB's Lane called AI a significant contributor to euro-area growth**. Central banks now model AI as a growth input, not a novelty. **The "AI is just hype" argument has lost its institutional defenders** — the debate is now about distribution, not existence.

Elsewhere: Micron's 42-year compounding shows **hardware winners are patient-capital stories**, and a **"molecule highway" membrane** that speeds water transport shows industrial efficiency, not chatbots, is where much of the real engineering is happening. All sources are listed below.
